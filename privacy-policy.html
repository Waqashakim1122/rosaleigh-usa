Personal Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our Services, we may collect:

  • Contact details: name, billing and delivery address, email address, phone number
  • Order and transaction information: items purchased, order history, refunds, exchanges. We do not store full card numbers or payment details — all payment information is processed securely by our payment processor and is never visible to or stored by Rosaleigh
  • Account information: username, password (encrypted), preferences, wishlist items
  • Communications: contents of messages sent to us via email, contact forms, or social media
  • Device and usage data: basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, and operating system, collected through essential website functionality
  • Marketing preferences: your opt-in/out choices for email marketing

We do not intentionally collect sensitive information (such as health information) unless you voluntarily provide it in a customer support message (for example, describing a skin reaction). We will only use such information to address your enquiry and will handle it with extra care.

How We Collect Personal Information

  • Directly from you when you create an account, place an order, subscribe to marketing, contact us, or enter a promotion
  • Automatically through essential cookies and similar technologies when you browse our website (for basic site functionality only — we do not use third-party analytics or advertising tracking tools)
  • From our e-commerce platform provider, which processes order and payment data on our behalf

Your Privacy Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to know what personal information we collect, request access to or a copy of your data, request correction of inaccurate data, request deletion of your data, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do not sell personal information). If you are a California resident, these rights are provided under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended. Residents of other states may have similar rights under applicable state privacy laws.

To exercise any of these rights, please email us at info@rosaleigh.us with "Privacy Request" in the subject line. We may ask you to verify your identity before processing your request, and we will respond within a reasonable time as required by applicable law.

You also have the right to withdraw consent at any time (for example, by unsubscribing from marketing emails) without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law:

  • Order records: 6 years (for tax and accounting purposes)
  • Customer account information: until you delete your account or after 3 years of inactivity
  • Marketing consent: until you withdraw consent
  • Customer service communications: 2 years after resolution

After the retention period, your data will be securely deleted or anonymized. We do not store any payment card details — all payment transactions are processed directly by our payment processor through secure, encrypted (SSL) connections.

Children's Privacy

Our Services are not intended for individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Security

We take the security of your personal data seriously and implement appropriate technical and organizational measures, including SSL encryption for all data transmitted between your browser and our website, PCI DSS compliant payment processing (we never see, handle, or store your card details), access controls so only authorized personnel can access order information, and regular security reviews of our website and systems. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

International Visitors

Our Services are hosted and operated in the United States. If you are accessing our Services from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States, where data protection laws may differ from those in your country.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will post the revised version on this page and update the "Last updated" date. For significant changes, we will provide a prominent notice on our website or by email.
