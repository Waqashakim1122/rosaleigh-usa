Our Ingredients

Simple, Natural & Biocompatible

Every ingredient in a Rosaleigh jar serves an active biological purpose. We refuse fillers, emulsifiers, parabens, synthetic fragrances, and cheap mineral oils. Only nutrient-dense ingredients that your skin instantly recognizes.
